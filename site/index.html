how it works

Hold Right Shift

Anywhere. In iTerm, in Cursor, mid-argument with your teammate. The overlay appears wherever your mouse is.

PRs sorted by who's actively being reviewed, with scores, review counts, and your CI runs on the side.

Release, breathe

Let go to hide. Click to open a PR, or hit ↻ to re-trigger a review like the tiny chaos gremlin you are.

faq

Does it spy on me?

Only mildly. It reads your open PRs through your existing gh login and judges them silently. Technically that's less surveillance than your code reviewer does.

Why Right Shift specifically?

Left Shift was already taken — by caps lock pretending to be a shift key. Right Shift had nothing better to do.

Does it work on Linux / Windows?

No, and we're deeply sorry. On those platforms it compiles to a single tear rolling down your terminal emulator's cheek.

Is it free? What's the catch?

Free, open source, no account, no telemetry. The catch is you'll finally have to look at your own CI failures instead of blaming the flaky test.

How do I update?

It checks GitHub Releases itself and can install updates with one click — SHA-256 verified, signature checked, swap safe. We did the boring part so you can keep vibing.
